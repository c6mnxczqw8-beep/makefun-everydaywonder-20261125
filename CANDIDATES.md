# Everyday wonder candidates (draft pass)

One sentence each, at most 200 characters, on a small moment
of wonder in everyday life. The judge rewards surprise.

1. Kettle applause: "The kettle clicks off at the exact
   moment the rain starts, and for one second the kitchen
   sounds like applause." -- synesthetic and small; the
   wonder is a coincidence of timing.
2. Train door: "A stranger held the train door for nobody in
   particular, just in case someone was running -- and
   somebody was." -- the wonder is a kindness aimed at a
   person who did not exist yet, arriving on cue.
3. First snow: "The first snow of the year makes the whole
   city walk slower, as if everyone agreed at once to be
   somewhere softer." -- gentle, familiar.
4. Cat sunbeam: "The cat found the one square of sunlight in
   the flat and is lying in it like a rumour of summer." --
   charming, animal-warm.
5. Escalator: "Two strangers on the escalator hummed the
   same song, noticed, and finished it together, quietly."
   -- a small duet; lovely, slightly staged.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The judge rewards surprise, and
candidate 2's wonder has a plot: a kindness aimed at a
person who did not exist yet -- a door held for nobody, just
in case -- and then, on cue, the somebody arrives. The
kettle (candidate 1) is a prettier image, but the train door
is the one that makes a reader believe the world is
occasionally arranged kindly on purpose.
